Epoxy resin science, minus the guesswork.

Five calculators. Each one takes what you know and returns an exact answer. None of them is tied to a brand. Anything that depends on your product is an input you can edit, with the default stated and the reasoning footnoted.

  • Mix ratioResin and hardener in grams and millilitres, for any ratio, by weight or by volume.
  • Pour volumeRectangles, cylinders, and river tables, with wood displacement taken off and the A/B split.
  • Deep-pour plannerSafe layer count, volume per layer, and a recoat schedule. Over-depth pours are refused.
  • Pigment loadingExact grams of mica, dye, or alcohol ink, with a hard-cap warning.
  • Project costCost per project and per layer from what you actually paid, in any pack size.

Safety

  • Work in a ventilated space. Wear an organic-vapour respirator, nitrile gloves, and eye protection. Uncured epoxy is a skin sensitiser, and an allergy builds with every exposure.
  • Runaway exotherm is a documented fire risk. Never pour deeper than your product is rated for.
  • Not food-safe unless the specific product is certified for food contact and fully cured to its data sheet.
