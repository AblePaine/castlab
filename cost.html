Notes

  1. "Pack" means the combined A + B volume, so a "1 gallon kit" is 1 gallon of mixed epoxy. Some kits are named after the size of Part A alone, or sold by weight. If yours is, enter the real combined volume. The data sheet or the label on each bottle will have it.
  2. The margin covers resin left in cups and on sticks, small leaks, and wood soak-in. There's no standard figure. Common advice runs from 5 to 15%. The exact-volume cost is shown on its own so you can see what the margin adds.
  3. This covers resin only. Pigment, tape, mold release, sandpaper, and finish are extra.

Safety

  • Work in a ventilated space. Wear an organic-vapour respirator, nitrile gloves, and eye protection. Uncured epoxy is a skin sensitiser, and an allergy builds with every exposure.
  • Runaway exotherm is a documented fire risk. Don't save money by pouring deeper than the product is rated for. Use the deep-pour planner.
  • Not food-safe unless the specific product is certified for food contact and fully cured to its data sheet.
